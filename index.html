The vision

Technology should adapt to you.

iX OS is being planned around a consistent core and interfaces that respond to screen size, connected displays, and the input methods available on a device.

↗

Adaptive by design

Move between compact, touch-first layouts and larger desktop-style workspaces without treating every device as a separate product.

◌

Glass-inspired UI

Frosted surfaces, subtle highlights, restrained motion, and CGL+ yellow accents — an original visual language, not a copy of another platform.

⇄

Connected experiences

The proposed iX Share direction focuses on intentional, secure sharing of files, links, and text across supported devices.

Built around context

Small screen or full workspace.

The goal is a coherent experience that changes its layout and interaction model to suit the hardware in front of you.

01

Phone

Reachable controls, clear hierarchy, and touch-first navigation.

02

Tablet

Room for side-by-side content, flexible panels, and multitasking concepts.

03

Desktop and external displays

A workspace designed for pointer, keyboard, larger windows, and connected screens where supported.

04

Accessibility and user choice

Respect system settings, input capabilities, and explicit user preferences rather than relying on screen size alone.

Project status
